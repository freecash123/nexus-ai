Principles

What we stand for.

Ten founding principles that shape every decision we make.

01
Intelligence should be transparent

Every conclusion has a traceable path. Ask "why?" and get a real answer.

02
Memory is a right, not a feature

Your memories are yours. Delete anything, anytime, permanently.

03
Agency requires trust

Every autonomous action has a confirmation gate. You are always in control.

04
Privacy is not optional

End-to-end encryption by default. Never trained on your private data without opt-in.

05
AI should be multimodal by nature

Voice, gesture, images, and text — all at once, like humans.

06
Creation is a human right

AI amplifies human creativity, never replaces it. You are the artist.

07
Learning never stops

Adapts to you over time, transparently. Reset or disable at any time.

08
Technology should serve everyone

Accessibility-first. Multi-language. Offline mode. Broad access pricing.

09
Open ecosystems beat walled gardens

Open plugin architecture. Deep integrations. Always exportable data.

10
Success = human flourishing

Measured by tasks completed, things learned, time saved — not engagement.
